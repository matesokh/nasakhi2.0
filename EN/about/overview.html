Shortly About Us Who we are and what services we offer

Summary

Our project team is comprised of highly qualified specialists with extensive and diverse experience in civil and industrial design. This experience allows us to manage processes smoothly and deliver innovative, optimal, and client-oriented solutions.

Our expertise includes the execution of the structural part of projects using modern software such as AutoCAD (for engineering drawings), Lira (for structural analysis), Revit (for BIM modeling), and others — ensuring the structural part is completed with quality and in full compliance with architectural requirements.

Responsibility, adherence to the highest safety standards, and continuous development are integral parts of our company philosophy.

Main Areas of Focus

Our main areas cover civil and industrial projects, which includes the structural design of multi-Storey residential buildings, Individual houses, Reconstruction and other commercial or Public Buildings.

We also offer Supervision on site and consulting services to ensure quality execution of projects, based on the developed drawings.
